GoodNod 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

GoodNod 没有账户，不联网，不含广告、统计或崩溃分析工具。你的记录只保存在你的 iPhone 上，GoodNod 不会把它们发送到任何地方。

在 iPhone 上保存的内容

  • 每晚的记录：时间和时区、问到的问题和你的回答（是、不是或没有回答）、由回答得出的心情、用点头还是屏幕按钮回答、使用的语言，以及这次记录是否完整结束。
  • 语音留言：只有在记录结束时你点头接受，才会录音，最长 30 秒。
  • 设置：语言、声音、记录时长，以及每日提醒的开关和时间。

AirPods 的头部动作只在记录进行时用来识别点头和摇头，不会保存。

保存在哪里

记录和语音留言保存在 GoodNod 自己的文件夹里，设置保存在 GoodNod 的偏好设置里。GoodNod 不使用 iCloud 同步，也没有自己的服务器。如果你开启了 iCloud 备份或电脑备份，iOS 会像对待其他应用一样，把这些内容放进你自己的备份里。

什么会离开你的 iPhone

GoodNod 不会发送任何数据。

如果你通过 TestFlight 安装测试版，或在 iPhone 设置中选择与开发者共享分析数据，Apple 可能会把崩溃报告和你主动提交的反馈提供给开发者。这部分由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

权限

  • 运动与健身（AirPods 动作）：把点头当作“是”、摇头当作“不是”。不允许也能用，屏幕上会出现“是 / 不是 / 跳过”按钮。
  • 麦克风：只用于结束时可选的语音留言，第一次开始记录前询问。不允许的话，GoodNod 不会提供语音留言，其他功能照常。
  • 通知：只在你打开每日提醒时询问，用来在你设定的时间提醒你。提醒由 iPhone 在本机安排。

GoodNod 还使用后台音频，让锁屏后记录可以继续播放。

删除数据

  • 删除某一晚：打开那一晚的页面，点底部的“删除这一晚”。这一晚的回答和语音留言都会删除，无法恢复。
  • 删除全部：删除 GoodNod 应用，iPhone 上的所有记录和设置会一起删除。之前做过的 iCloud 或电脑备份里如果还有副本，需要在那份备份里删除。

儿童

GoodNod 没有账户，不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，会更新这个页面和上面的生效日期。

联系

请在 GitHub Issues 提问。Issues 是公开的，请不要写入个人信息或记录内容。

GoodNod Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

GoodNod has no account, does not use the network, and contains no ads, analytics or crash reporting tools. Your entries stay on your iPhone. GoodNod does not send them anywhere.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Each night's entry: the time and time zone, the questions asked and your answers (yes, no or no answer), the mood worked out from your answers, whether you answered by nodding or with the on-screen buttons, the language, and whether the session ran to the end.
  • Voice note: recorded only if you nod to accept the offer at the end of a session, up to 30 seconds.
  • Settings: language, voice, session length, and the daily reminder's on/off state and time.

Head movement from your AirPods is used only during a session to recognise nods and shakes. It is not saved.

Where it is stored

Entries and voice notes are kept in GoodNod's own folder, and settings in GoodNod's preferences. GoodNod does not use iCloud sync and has no server of its own. If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, iOS includes this data in your own backups, as it does for other apps.

What leaves your iPhone

GoodNod sends no data.

If you install a beta through TestFlight, or choose in iPhone Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass crash reports and feedback you choose to send to the developer. Apple handles that under Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Motion & Fitness (AirPods motion): to hear a nod as yes and a shake as no. Without it you can still answer with the on-screen Yes / No / Skip buttons.
  • Microphone: only for the optional voice note at the end of a session, asked before your first session. If you decline, GoodNod does not offer the voice note and everything else works.
  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on the daily reminder, to remind you at the time you set. The reminder is scheduled on your iPhone.

GoodNod also uses background audio so a session keeps playing when the screen is locked.

Deleting your data

  • One night: open that night's page and tap "Delete this night" at the bottom. Its answers and voice note are deleted and cannot be recovered.
  • Everything: delete the GoodNod app. All entries and settings on your iPhone are deleted with it. Copies in earlier iCloud or computer backups have to be deleted from those backups.

Children

GoodNod has no account and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated.
