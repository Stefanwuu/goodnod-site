GoodNod 帮助与支持

获取帮助

遇到问题或有建议，请在 GitHub Issues 提出。请写明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、AirPods 型号和发生了什么。Issues 是公开的，请不要写入个人信息或记录内容。

需要什么

  • iPhone，iOS 17 或更高版本。
  • 支持头部追踪的 AirPods：AirPods 3、AirPods 4、AirPods 5、AirPods Pro 或 AirPods Max（见 Apple 的说明）。
  • 没有这类 AirPods 也能用：屏幕上会出现“是 / 不是 / 跳过”按钮。

常见问题

点头没有反应？
确认 AirPods 已连接并戴好。如果之前没有允许动作权限，请到 设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 运动与健身 打开 GoodNod。头部动作不可用时，声音会提示一次，屏幕上会出现“是 / 不是 / 跳过”按钮，你可以接着回答；点头恢复后按钮会自动隐藏。

怎么中途停止？
按住屏幕一秒半。

锁屏后还会继续吗？
会。GoodNod 使用后台音频，锁屏后记录继续播放。

语音留言怎么用？
记录结束时会问你要不要留言，点头接受后开始录音，最长 30 秒，也可以点“结束留言”提前结束。没有允许麦克风时不会提供留言。

怎么设置每日提醒？
在 GoodNod 的设置里打开“提醒”并选择时间。第一次打开时 iPhone 会询问通知权限。

支持哪些语言？
简体中文和英文。默认跟随 iPhone 的语言，也可以在 GoodNod 的设置里选择。

怎么删除记录？
打开那一晚的页面，点底部的“删除这一晚”。删除 GoodNod 应用会删除所有记录。

我的记录会上传吗？
不会。GoodNod 不联网，记录只保存在你的 iPhone 上。详见隐私政策。

GoodNod Support

Getting help

For problems or suggestions, please open an issue on GitHub Issues. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, AirPods model and what happened. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details and the contents of your entries.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • AirPods with head tracking: AirPods 3, AirPods 4, AirPods 5, AirPods Pro or AirPods Max (see Apple's page).
  • Without them you can still use GoodNod: Yes / No / Skip buttons appear on screen.

Questions

My nods aren't picked up.
Check that your AirPods are connected and in your ears. If you didn't allow motion access, turn GoodNod on in Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness. When head motion isn't available, the voice says so once and Yes / No / Skip buttons appear so you can keep answering; they hide again once nods come through.

How do I stop a session?
Press and hold the screen for one and a half seconds.

Does it keep going with the screen locked?
Yes. GoodNod uses background audio, so the session keeps playing when the screen is locked.

How does the voice note work?
At the end of a session you are asked whether you want to leave a note. Nod to accept and recording starts, for up to 30 seconds; tap "End note" to finish early. The note is not offered if microphone access is off.

How do I set a daily reminder?
Turn on Reminder in GoodNod's settings and pick a time. iPhone asks for notification permission the first time.

Which languages are there?
English and Simplified Chinese. GoodNod follows your iPhone's language, or you can choose one in its settings.

How do I delete an entry?
Open that night's page and tap "Delete this night" at the bottom. Deleting the GoodNod app deletes all entries.

Are my entries uploaded?
No. GoodNod does not use the network, and entries stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
